Rider Terms & Conditions

1. Who we are

One Way is an independent, private driver service operated by IJen Wang (“we”, “the driver”). It is not a taxi company, a rideshare platform, or a transportation network company. One vehicle seats up to passengers.

2. Booking and payment

Send a booking request through this website with a pickup date and time (at least 2 hours ahead), route, passenger count, bag count, your name and mobile phone (email optional). The fare shown is the fare for that route, passenger count, and any extra bags. It covers the driver’s full loop and typical tolls. A request is not a confirmed booking: the booking is confirmed only when the driver confirms it by email or text. There is no online payment.

3. Cancellation

  • Free cancellation more than hours before pickup.
  • Cancellation within hours of pickup: % of the fare.
  • No-show (rider not at pickup and unreachable after the free waiting time): % of the fare.
  • Cancel by phone, text or email.

4. Waiting time

  • Airport pickups: minutes free after your flight lands.
  • All other pickups: minutes free from the booked time.
  • After that: $ per minute.

5. Passengers and luggage

Maximum passengers. About bags per passenger are fine (large + carry-on counted together). Soft capacity about bags per passenger — one car only; oversized loads may not fit. Tell us about oversized items (skis, golf bags, strollers) when booking. There is no per-bag fee at this time.

6. Flight delays

For airport pickups, please give your flight number. We track the flight and adjust the pickup for delays. Rescheduling because of a flight delay or cancellation is free.

7. Child safety seats

New Jersey law requires children to ride in an appropriate car seat or booster. You must request child seats in advance when booking, with each child’s age and weight. We cannot carry a child who needs a seat that was not requested.

8. Conduct in the vehicle

No smoking, vaping, cannabis use or open containers of alcohol in the vehicle. The driver may end a ride for unsafe or abusive behavior.

9. Damage and cleaning

If a rider damages or soils the vehicle, a cleaning or damage fee of up to $ may be charged. We will provide photos.

10. Times, liability and disclaimer

Pickup and travel times are estimates. We are not responsible for missed flights or connections caused by traffic, weather, road closures, airport or airline delays, or rider lateness. Please arrive at the airport by your airline’s recommended time and allow extra time. To the fullest extent the law allows, our total liability for any ride is limited to the fare paid for that ride. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under New Jersey law.

11. Privacy

Your name, phone, email, flight number and trip details are used only to schedule and provide your ride, to contact you about it, and to offer repeat-rider discounts. We do not sell or share your data, except when the law requires it.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey.

13. Contact

Questions, changes or cancellations: call or text the driver at the phone number given in your booking confirmation, or email customerservice@robotkitchen.us.

一、服務提供者

One Way 是由 IJen Wang（「本服務」、「司機」）經營的獨立私人司機服務，不是計程車公司、共乘平台或交通網路公司。車輛一台，最多可載 位乘客。

二、預約與付款

請在本網站送出預約申請，填寫上車日期與時間（至少提前 2 小時）、路線、人數、行李、姓名、手機號碼及電子郵件。顯示的車資就是該路線和人數的車資，已包含司機全程來回及一般過路費。送出申請不代表預約成立，司機以電子郵件或簡訊確認後才算成立。本服務不提供線上付款。

三、取消政策

  • 上車時間 小時前取消：免費。
  • 上車前 小時內取消：車資的 %。
  • 未到（免費等候時間過後乘客未出現且聯絡不上）：車資的 %。
  • 請以電話、簡訊或電子郵件取消。

四、等候時間

  • 機場接機：班機落地後免費等候 分鐘。
  • 其他上車地點：自預約時間起免費等候 分鐘。
  • 超過後每分鐘 $。

五、人數與行李

最多 人。約每位乘客 件行李即可（大行李與登機箱合併計算）。建議上限約每位 件——只有一台車，超大件可能裝不下。超大物品（滑雪板、高爾夫球袋、嬰兒車）請在預約時告知。目前不另收行李件數費。

六、班機延誤

機場接機請提供航班號。我們會追蹤班機，延誤時調整接機時間。因班機延誤或取消而改期，不另收費。

七、兒童安全座椅

依紐澤西州法律，兒童必須使用合適的安全座椅或增高墊。如需兒童座椅，請在預約時事先提出，並告知每位兒童的年齡和體重。未事先提出而需要座椅的兒童，我們無法載送。

八、車內規範

車內禁止吸菸、電子菸、使用大麻及攜帶已開封的酒類。乘客有危險或辱罵行為時，司機可以終止行程。

九、損壞與清潔

乘客造成車輛損壞或髒污時，可能收取最高 $ 的清潔或損壞費，並會附上照片。

十、時間、責任與免責聲明

上車及行車時間均為預估。因交通、天氣、封路、機場或航空公司延誤、或乘客遲到而錯過班機或轉機，本服務不負責任。請依航空公司建議的時間抵達機場，並預留額外時間。在法律允許的最大範圍內，本服務對任何一趟行程的總責任以該趟已付車資為限。依紐澤西州法律不得限制的責任，不受本條款限制。

十一、隱私

您的姓名、電話、電子郵件、航班號及行程資料只用於安排和提供行程、與您聯絡，以及提供回頭客優惠。除法律要求外，我們不會出售或分享您的資料。

十二、準據法

本條款以美國紐澤西州法律為準據法。

十三、聯絡方式

如有問題、要變更或取消預約，請撥打或傳簡訊到預約確認中提供的電話，或寄信至 customerservice@robotkitchen.us。
