Our story

A room that faces the park, and a kitchen that faces the season.

Meridian Court is an invented brand for Robot Kitchen. The site shape — tasting menus, narrative, reservations, private dining — follows how many upscale restaurants organize their guest journey. Nothing here is affiliated with any real restaurant.

We cook from a short market list. Guests choose a plant path or a mixed path at two points in the Court Tasting. The lounge stays open for walk-in counter seats when the room is full.
